title=Play it! Playable musical instruments
description=Found a guitar in someone's living room and wished you could do more with it than smack a zombie? Or maybe you just want to annoy the rest of your base at 3 AM? Yeah, me too.
description=
description=[b]Play it![/b] makes the instruments you find actually playable. Paste in a song, press Play, and your survivor plays it note by note - and everyone nearby hears it. Including the zombies.
description=
description=[h2]Playing Music[/h2]
description=Right-click a [b]guitar, violin, trumpet, harmonica, flute, saxophone or keytar[/b] and choose [b]Play[/b]. Your survivor takes it in hand and the music window opens.
description=
description=Paste a song, pick your [b]part[/b], set the [b]BPM[/b] and [b]volume[/b], and press [b]Play[/b]. You can walk while you play, pause and resume right where you stopped, and [b]Save[/b] your favourite songs - they're kept on your computer, so they follow you to every character and every server.
description=
description=Running, getting hit, getting grabbed or pressing [b]Esc[/b] pauses the song. Close the window mid-song and a small widget stays on screen, so you can keep an eye on the road instead of the sheet music.
description=
description=[h2]Pianos, Organs and Drums[/h2]
description=Found a piano in someone's living room? Right-click a [b]piano[/b], [b]grand piano[/b], [b]church organ[/b] or a [b]drum[/b] and choose [b]Play[/b]. Your survivor walks over and the music window opens - everything works just like the hand-held instruments. Sit on a stool next to it if you like, or play standing. Walk away and the song pauses.
description=
description=A drum kit is every [b]kick, snare and tom drum[/b] within reach - the snare comes with a [b]hi-hat[/b] and the tom with a [b]crash cymbal[/b]. Drum parts hit the right drum for every beat. Play any other song on the kit and the lowest notes hit the kick, then the toms, the snare and the hi-hat, and the very highest notes crash the cymbal. Missing a drum? Those notes stay silent. Oh, and you'll need a [b]drumstick[/b] in your inventory.
description=
description=[h2]Where Do Songs Come From?[/h2]
description=Songs are written in [b]ABC notation[/b] - the same format LOTRO and Starbound bards use, and there are hundreds of thousands of tunes online ready to paste.
description=
description=Got a MIDI file instead? Drop it into the [url=https://playit-midi.vercel.app/][b]Play it! MIDI converter[/b][/url]. It splits the song into parts - piano, guitar, brass, drums and so on - and gives you one text with all of them. Everything happens in your browser, nothing gets uploaded.
description=
description=[h2]Playing Together[/h2]
description=Got friends who are as bad at music as you are? Start a band! Everyone pastes the same song and picks their own part.
description=
description=Open your music window, right-click another musician and choose [b]Play along[/b]. If they haven't started yet, press Play and wait - when they start, everyone gets a countdown and comes in on the same beat. If they're already playing, you come in at the start of the next bar.
description=
description=[h2]Practice Makes Perfect[/h2]
description=Playing trains the new [b]Music[/b] skill. A beginner hits a wrong note about [b]half the time[/b], plays out of tune and loses the beat. Somewhere around [b]level 4-5[/b] it starts sounding like actual music, and by [b]level 10[/b] you're practically flawless.
description=
description=Being [b]drunk[/b] or [b]panicked[/b] doesn't help either. [i]Turns out a horde banging on the door is bad for your rhythm.[/i]
description=
description=[h2]Everyone's a Critic[/h2]
description=Music is real noise. Zombies hear it from up to [b]45 tiles[/b] away with a trumpet at full volume - great for luring a horde away from a building you want to loot, terrible for a quiet night in. Turn the volume down to play it safe.
description=
description=Listening to music [b]cures boredom[/b] for you and everyone in earshot - the better you play, the faster it works. Play badly, though, and every wrong note makes your audience a little more [b]stressed[/b] and [b]unhappy[/b].
description=
description=Wild animals run from the racket. Tame animals that trust you might just wander over to listen. [i]Even the cows have better taste than your neighbours.[/i]
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Seven hand-held instruments plus pianos, the church organ and drums, using real recorded notes.
description=[*]Paste songs in ABC notation, or convert your own MIDI files with the [url=https://playit-midi.vercel.app/]Play it! MIDI converter[/url].
description=[*]Play along with other survivors - one song, one beat, every part.
description=[*]Save and load your songs, pause and resume, play while walking.
description=[*]New Music skill - from tone-deaf to virtuoso.
description=[*]Zombies come to the concert. Plan accordingly.
description=[*]Music cures boredom... unless you're bad at it.
description=[*]Works in multiplayer - everyone hears the same song, wrong notes and all.
description=[*]Sandbox options for the skill, blunders, annoyance and noise.
description=[*]Your fellow survivors may grow to hate you.
description=[/list]
